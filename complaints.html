Complaints & feedback

We take your concerns seriously.

If you are unhappy with our service, workmanship, sales process, communication or a solar project, we want to hear from you and have the opportunity to resolve the issue.

Our commitment

Fair, prompt and transparent complaint handling

Your Sparky Mate NQ aims to handle complaints fairly, promptly and respectfully. We will listen to your concerns, keep appropriate records, investigate the matter objectively and work with you toward a reasonable outcome.

NETCC status: We are currently progressing our application to become a New Energy Tech Consumer Code (NETCC) Approved Seller. We are developing our customer processes with the NETCC standards in mind. We do not claim to be NETCC approved unless and until formal approval is granted.

1
Tell us what happenedYou can make a complaint by phone or email. Clearly explain the issue, what service or project it relates to and the outcome you are seeking.
2
Keep a recordWhere practical, put your complaint and follow-up correspondence in writing so both you and we have a clear record of the concerns raised.
3
We acknowledge and record itWe will acknowledge your complaint promptly and record the relevant details so the matter can be properly followed up.
4
We investigate fairlyWe will review relevant job, quote, contract, installation and communication records and consider any supporting information you provide.
5
Safety concerns are prioritisedIf your complaint involves an immediate electrical or other safety concern, tell us straight away so we can prioritise the issue and provide appropriate advice or action.
6
We keep you informedIf the matter cannot be resolved immediately, we will keep you informed of progress and let you know if we need more information from you.
7
Outcome and reasonsWe will explain the outcome of our review and, where appropriate, any corrective action, remedy or next steps.
8
Further reviewIf you are not satisfied with the outcome, contact us again and explain why. We will review the matter and advise you of any appropriate external avenue that may apply.
What to include

Help us investigate quickly

Please include your name and preferred contact details, the relevant job or project details, dates where known, a clear description of the issue, the outcome you are seeking, and copies of any useful documents, photographs, quotes, invoices or correspondence.

If you need assistance communicating your complaint, let us know and we will make reasonable efforts to help.

Scope

What you can complain about

Complaints can relate to matters including sales and marketing, quotations or contracts, installation and safety, workmanship, system performance, customer service, warranties, after-sales support or other services provided by Your Sparky Mate NQ.

Privacy & records

How we handle your information

We will handle complaint information responsibly and use or disclose it only as reasonably required to investigate, respond to and resolve the complaint, or where required or permitted by law.

Relevant records may be retained so we can monitor outcomes, identify recurring issues and improve our services.
